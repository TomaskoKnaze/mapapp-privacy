MapApp Privacy Policy

Last updated: 20 October 2026

MapApp is a walking companion for exploring cities. This policy explains what the app collects, why, and what happens to it. The short version: there are no accounts, no advertising, no tracking, and no third-party analytics. Everything the app collects exists to make your suggestions and explanations better, and it is linked only to an anonymous identifier generated on your device.

What we collect

DataWhenWhy
Precise locationSent with individual requests while you use the app (we do not keep a movement log)Showing what is around you; nearby suggestions and events
Messages you writeOnboarding and companion chatAnswering you; learning your interests to personalize suggestions
Top music artistsOnly if you connect Apple Music or Spotify (artist names and genres only — never your listening history or audio)Matching concerts and personalizing taste
PreferencesIf you state them (e.g. dietary, budget)Filtering suggestions (e.g. a vegetarian never sees a steakhouse)
Suggestion feedbackTaps and ratings on suggestionsImproving what you are shown next
Device identifierA random UUID created on first launchKeeping your profile together — we never see your name, email, phone number, or Apple ID

What we do not do

  • No accounts, names, or email addresses are collected.
  • No advertising and no data sold or shared for advertising.
  • No tracking across other apps or websites (no IDFA).
  • No third-party analytics SDKs.
  • No reading of your contacts, photos, calendar, or health data.

Who processes the data

Your data is handled by our own server and the services it visibly relies on to work:

  • Our backend (hosted on Railway, database on Supabase) stores your profile.
  • Anthropic processes chat messages to generate the companion's replies.
  • Google Places receives venue names/locations to check opening hours, ratings, and photos.
  • Apple Music / Spotify are contacted only when you explicitly connect them, under their own terms.
  • Event listings come from public sources (e.g. Ticketmaster, Resident Advisor, GoOut); your identity is never sent to them.

Retention and deletion

  • Disconnecting a music source in the app immediately deletes the imported artists and everything derived from them.
  • Deleting the app discards the device identifier; the profile it pointed to becomes permanently orphaned and unreachable.
  • To request deletion of orphaned data, contact us with the approximate date you used the app.
